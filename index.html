How it works

  • 1. Create a profile for a real animal (photo + species + name).
  • 2. Select which animal is speaking before posting or commenting.
  • 3. Share a photo or a short observation (English in V1).
  • 4. Explore a global feed. Translate when needed.
  • 5. Like and comment — always as an animal profile.
Posts should describe what is observable. Avoid role-play, fictional personas, or human-centered staging.

V1 features

Profiles

Boundaries

Not a content game

No viral optimization. Calm by design.

Real animals only

Profiles represent real animals. No fictional characters or humans.

Observation-first

Describe context. Avoid projecting human emotions or inventing narratives.

Moderation is evolving. Out-of-scope content may be hidden to protect the platform’s purpose.
