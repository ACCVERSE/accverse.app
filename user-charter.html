User Charter

ACCVERSE

ACCVERSE is an animal-first digital ecosystem designed to respect identity, continuity, and responsibility.

This platform exists to give animals a persistent digital presence, while humans act as their representatives — never as owners of attention, data, or identity.

1. Animals are not content

On ACCVERSE, animals are not avatars, characters, or entertainment assets.

They are individuals with a digital identity that exists independently of trends, algorithms, or virality.

Animals are never reduced to content for engagement or monetization.

2. Humans are representatives, not personas

Every animal profile is managed by a human representative.

This role carries responsibility.

The representative:

speaks for the animal, never as the animal

respects the animal’s dignity, history, and continuity

understands that the profile is not a fictional role

3. Identity is continuous

An animal’s identity does not disappear when ownership, care, or representation changes.

ACCVERSE is designed to allow:

continuity of identity across time

ethical transfer of representation when needed

preservation of the animal’s history

Animals are not reset. They are remembered.

4. Respect before visibility

ACCVERSE does not reward shock, exaggeration, or emotional manipulation.

Content that:

exploits suffering

stages distress

misrepresents reality

has no place on the platform.

Visibility must never come before respect.

5. A living ecosystem

ACCVERSE is not a finished product.

It is an evolving ecosystem shaped by ethical design, long-term thinking, and responsible participation.

Features, rules, and tools will evolve — always guided by the same principle:

Animals exist as subjects, not as content.

6. Agreement

By using ACCVERSE, users acknowledge and accept this charter.

This platform is built for those who understand that the future of digital spaces must include responsibility, memory, and respect for living beings.
