Help & Contact

Clarity, continuity, and respectful testing.

ACCVERSE is an animal-first digital ecosystem. Our priority is clarity, continuity, and respect — for animals and for the humans who represent them. This section exists to help you understand the platform, report issues, and contact us when necessary.

When to contact us
  • — encounter a technical issue
  • — have a question about how ACCVERSE works
  • — need clarification about rules or principles
  • — wish to report inappropriate or out-of-scope content
  • — want to provide constructive feedback during testing

Platform status

ACCVERSE is currently evolving and may be: — in private testing — in limited access — in phased deployment Features may change as the platform grows.

What ACCVERSE is not

ACCVERSE does not provide:

  • medical advice
  • legal advice
  • emergency support

Decisions concerning animals always remain the responsibility of their human representatives and must comply with local laws and regulations.
